# Quosparr brand assets

The Quosparr mark is a compact right-facing sparrow. Its open Q-shaped wing uses true transparent negative space: the left tips remain deliberately separated, while the lower-right feather seam passes through the body edge.

## Files

- `quosparr-logo.svg`: scalable colour symbol master with subtle chestnut and apricot shading
- `quosparr-logo-4096.png` and `quosparr-logo-1024.png`: transparent raster handoff files
- `quosparr-logo-small-black.svg` and `quosparr-logo-small-white.svg`: simplified one-colour small-size masters
- `quosparr-logo-horizontal.svg`: symbol plus outlined wordmark
- `png/`: general raster sizes
- `mono/`: 16, 32 and 64 px black/white assets
- `preview/quosparr-brand-preview.png`: light, dark, monochrome and horizontal review sheet

## Palette

- Quosparr Chestnut `#B3582D`: primary mark and application theme colour
- Apricot `#E9A65C`: belly and wing surface
- Deep Brown `#553C30`: beak

The colour master adds soft highlights and shadows to these base colours for a shallow sculpted appearance; the black and white small-size masters remain flat.

## Use

Keep clear space around the symbol equal to the eye diameter. Use the colour symbol at 64 px or larger and the simplified one-colour symbol below 64 px. Do not close the Q's left opening, shorten its through-cut feather seam, stretch the artwork, rotate it or place it on a low-contrast background.

The horizontal wordmark is outlined from Arial Rounded MT Bold supplied with macOS. It contains no live text and does not require the font to render. The typeface is proprietary system software; confirm the applicable Apple/Microsoft font licence before commercial trademark deployment. The symbol itself is constructed from original SVG paths and embeds no raster artwork.
